Section 01

Your Right to Delete Your Account

You may request deletion of your WrindhaOS account and associated personal data at any time, whether or not you have an active subscription, in line with Google Play's account and data deletion requirements.

Section 02

How to Request Deletion

  1. In-app: Settings → Account → Delete Account, which submits a deletion request.
  2. Web: via the account deletion request form at wrindhaos.in/delete-account, accessible without needing to install the app.
  3. By email: write to [SUPPORT EMAIL — e.g., support@wrindhaos.in] from your registered email address, or provide your registered phone number, so we can verify your identity.
Section 03

What Happens After You Request Deletion

Your request moves through the following stages, tracked against your account:

Requested Scheduled Processing Completed
  • Requested — your request is received and logged.
  • Scheduled — your request is confirmed and queued for processing, generally within 7 days of the request unless you cancel it.
  • Processing — your account and associated personal data are being permanently removed from our production systems.
  • Completed — deletion is finished. You will no longer be able to sign in with your previous credentials.

You may cancel a pending deletion request before it reaches the Processing stage by contacting [SUPPORT EMAIL — e.g., support@wrindhaos.in] or, where available, through the in-app cancellation option.

Section 04

What Gets Deleted

Upon completion, we permanently delete: your profile (name, email, phone number, profile image), authentication identities, journal entries, habits and habit history, goals and milestones, subjects/units/topics and study session history, to-dos and matrix tasks, calendar and timetable data, expense and budget records, notification settings, and consent records tied to your account.

Section 05

What May Be Retained, and Why

Consistent with our Data Retention &Deletion Policy, we may retain limited records after deletion where required by law or legitimate business need, including:

  • Billing and transaction records required for tax, accounting, or audit purposes (retained per statutory limitation periods).
  • Records reasonably necessary to investigate fraud, abuse, or a violation of our Acceptable Use Policy.
  • Aggregate, de-identified analytics that no longer identify you personally.
  • Support ticket records needed to resolve an open dispute at the time of deletion.

Any such retained data is kept only as long as necessary for the specific purpose and is not used to re-activate or reconstruct your deleted account.

Section 06

Effect on Subscriptions

Deleting your account does not automatically cancel an active Google Play subscription.

Cancel your subscription separately via Google Play before or after requesting account deletion to stop future billing; see our Subscription Policy .

Section 07

Timeline

We aim to complete account deletion within 30 days of a confirmed (Scheduled) request, except where longer retention is required as described in Section 5.
