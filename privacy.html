Plain-language edition

Your data is for your productivity—not for sale.

This short policy explains how WrindhaOS handles information in the Android app and at wrindhaos.in.

Publisher: [WRINDHA OS]Effective:
Our core promise

We do not sell personal data, use private journal or productivity content for third-party ad training, or receive your card, UPI, or bank details.

01

What we collect

You provide

Name, email or phone, optional profile image, journal entries, habits, tasks, goals, study records, calendar items, budgets, expenses, support messages, and consent choices.

Collected automatically

Feature usage, study and task counts, diagnostics, app version, IP and user-agent records, plus subscription status and order metadata from Google Play.

02

Why we use it

  • Run your account and sync features across devices.
  • Send reminders and notifications you choose to enable.
  • Provide support, resolve grievances, and prevent misuse.
  • Create de-identified product analytics and meet legal duties.

Processing is based mainly on your consent, the service you ask us to provide, and legal obligations. You can withdraw consent by deleting individual content or your account, subject to required retention.

03

Who can see it & how it is protected

Owner-only database rules restrict private records to your account. Journal content is encrypted. Administrators use limited account, billing, support, and aggregate data to operate the service.

Necessary providers may process data for hosting, authentication, notifications, diagnostics, or Google Play billing. Data may also be disclosed when legally required.

04

Your choices and rights

Access and review your data.

Correct inaccurate details.

Delete records or your account.

Withdraw optional consent.

Manage notifications.

Raise a grievance.

Requests are acknowledged promptly and handled within applicable legal timelines. Children under 13 may not use WrindhaOS; users aged 13–17 require parent or guardian consent.

05

Retention, transfers & updates

Personal content is kept while your account is active and removed according to the Data Retention & Deletion Policy after deletion requests. Limited billing, consent, security, or legal records may be retained where required.

Some service providers may process data outside India using appropriate safeguards. Policy updates are posted at wrindhaos.in/privacy and, when material, communicated in the app.
