Section 01

Purpose

This register lists every third party that processes WrindhaOS user data on our behalf, or that we integrate with, and what each one does. It supports our Privacy Policy, our Google Play Data Safety declaration, and our obligations under the DPDP Act, 2023 to know and document our data-processing chain.

Section 03

Categories We Do Not Currently Share Data With

  • Advertising networks / ad-tech SDKs — not integrated as of this version.
  • Data brokers or resellers — never.
  • Third-party AI model training providers — never, for user-generated private content (journal, habits, etc.).
Section 04

Internal Access

Internal administrative access is role-based and limited to account metadata, subscription metadata, aggregate analytics, support-ticket handling, and account-status management. It does not extend to reading individual users' journal, habits, goals, or other owner-only data (see Security &Data Protection Statement, Section 3).
